# Week 1 — Initial Discovery

## 1. Facts

* Shared equipment
* Laptops
* Cameras
* Projectors
* Booking by email
* Spreadsheets
* Double-booking
* Manual checking
* Late returns

## 2. Assumptions

* Students can book
* Staff can book
* Equipment needs approval
* Users need availability

## 3. Unknowns

* Who can book?
* Booking duration?
* Return rules?
* Who approves bookings?

## 4. Stakeholders

* Students — easy booking
* Staff — equipment access
* Equipment staff — booking management
* Managers — resource control
* IT staff — system support

## 5. Goals

* Reduce double-booking
* Show availability
* Reduce manual work

## 6. Scope

### In scope

* Equipment booking
* Availability checking
* Booking management
* Return tracking

### Out of scope / not established

* Mobile app
* Payment system
* Specific database

## 7. Candidate Requirements

### Functional

* Equipment booking
* Availability checking
* Prevent double-booking
* Return time tracking

### Non-functional

* Security
* Reliability

## 8. Requirement Surgery

### R1: Easy to use

* Weak: vague
* Improved: Simple booking process

### R2: Secure

* Weak: vague
* Improved: Authorised users only

## 9. Reflection

* Facts vs assumptions
* Identify unknowns
* Understand stakeholders
* Improve vague requirements
